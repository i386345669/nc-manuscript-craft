## Study evidence

FlowLedger connects stable sensor identifiers across archived measurements, regional comparisons and requests for new measurements. The archive covers 620 sensor records from 12 sites. The current interface supports lookup by identifier, comparison of two documented recording periods and export of the underlying measurement rows. The identifier remains traceable throughout these actions.

A paired test of 18 records under the same new configuration showed similar reconstruction error for compact and full representations: mean absolute error 1.8 and 1.9 units, respectively. Their paired difference was −0.1 units (95% CI −0.4 to 0.2). The compact representation used 42% less storage. The older archive and this matched comparison have different processing versions. The same paired test did not evaluate the numerical interchangeability of those versions.

An event score fell from 0.54 to 0.22 after removal of one model input. A readout excluding the two directly dependent components also fell, from 0.46 to 0.25. At the same time, reconstructed coverage fell from 95% to 61%. All these observations are model outputs, not field interventions. They help identify which independent measurements could distinguish a changed event profile from loss of reconstructed coverage.

Across the 12 sites, compact and full representations had descriptive mean reference correlations of 0.66 and 0.64. The paired test did not resolve a difference. No user study, prospective deployment or cost-effectiveness evaluation was performed.
